About Pl4nck

Pl4nck helps quants in banks and asset managers work out what quantum computing really means for their models, and what to do about it today. It was built by a practising quant with a background in model validation, structured products and regulatory frameworks.

What it does

1

An honest verdict

For one use case at a time: where quantum computing stands, when it could matter, and which technology fits.

2

A no-regret roadmap

The steps from your current model to quantum readiness, each with observable criteria and an order of magnitude of the benefit.

3

A starter kit

For your next step: Python prototype, benchmarks, unit tests, test plan and a documentation outline aligned with SR 11-7. You pick the parts.

4

Follow-up questions

Hardware fit, the business case for management, what a validator would check, and the research to read.

The no-regret principle

For most finance workloads, a real quantum advantage needs error-corrected machines that are not expected before the 2030s. Waiting is still not neutral. The prerequisites are the same either way: clean data, classical benchmarks, variance reduction, reproducible runs and validated documentation. Any quantum algorithm will be measured against them, and they pay off on their own.

So every roadmap Pl4nck builds starts with steps worth doing even if quantum computing never delivers. Quantum comes last, with a dated go/no-go based on resource estimates rather than announcements.

  1. TodayYour current model, priced and documented
  2. BenchmarksClosed forms and reference values, errors reported
  3. EfficiencyVariance reduction, convergence study, AAD Greeks
  4. Model riskRicher models compared, findings closed
  5. Quantum readinessReduced case on a simulator, resource estimate, go/no-go

What it does not do

  • It does not touch your systems. Pl4nck does not run your models or connect to anything inside your firm.
  • It does not take your code or data. You describe your model family in generic terms; nothing is uploaded.
  • It does not replace validation. Outputs are a starting point, roughly 80% of the work. Your team reviews, adapts and validates them, and owns any regulatory sign-off.
  • It does not promise quantum advantage. If quantum computing is not the right answer for your use case, the verdict says so.
  • It does not sell hardware. Pl4nck is vendor-neutral, with no sponsored placements.
  • It does not cover post-quantum cryptography beyond a pointer. That migration is urgent, but it is a security programme, not a modelling one.

Who it is for

Quant R&D, model validation, front-office quants and structurers, risk managers, innovation teams, and managers planning skills and budget. No quantum background is needed: answers adapt to what you already know.

How it works

Answers are generated by an AI model (Claude, by Anthropic) guided by a curated reference base: the technology mapping and reference roadmaps, reviewed by a practitioner. AI answers can be wrong. Treat them as a well-informed first draft and check what matters.

Pl4nck is a free research prototype. With your consent, your answers and the conversation are stored for 12 months to improve it. Do not share confidential information. See the privacy notice.
