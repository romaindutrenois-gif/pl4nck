Privacy notice

Veqctor · last updated 29 October 2026

In short: this is a research prototype. With your consent, we store your answers to the intake questions, your conversation with the advisor and your optional feedback, to learn what quants find useful. Your email is only stored if you choose to give it. We use no cookies or trackers. Please do not share confidential information, code or data from your employer.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Phalanx Capital, 60 rue François Ier, 75008 Paris, France, which publishes Veqctor. Contact: romaind@phalanx-capital.eu.

We never ask for your name, your employer's name, positions, code or data. If you include personal or confidential information in your messages, it will be stored with the conversation: please don't.

Your answers are not used to make any automated decision about you, and we do not sell or share them for advertising.

Who processes the data for us

  • Anthropic (Claude API) generates the advisor's answers from your intake answers and messages. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic does not use API data to train its models by default and keeps it only for a limited period.
  • Vercel hosts the website and its server functions.
  • Upstash stores the sessions in a database.

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Transfers outside the EU rely on the safeguards these providers offer, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where applicable.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data, withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect processing done before), and object to or restrict processing. To do so, email romaind@phalanx-capital.eu with your session reference, shown at the bottom of the advisor page during your session. Without it, we cannot find an anonymous session; if you gave your email, you can use that instead.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you can lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL, or with the authority of your country.

Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Access to stored sessions is restricted to the controller. The service is a prototype and not designed for confidential information. See also the security page and the legal notice.

Changes

If this notice changes in a meaningful way, the date above will change and the new version will apply to new sessions.
