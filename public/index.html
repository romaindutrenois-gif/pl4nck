The road to useful quantum finance

The theory is sound and the applications will be transformative, but quantum advantage is not for today.

  1. Today to 2028Demonstrated so far Dozens logical qubitsHundreds to a few thousand physical Small pilots: portfolio optimisation, toy option pricing. No proven advantage.
  2. 2029 to 2030Vendor roadmaps 100 to 200 logical qubitsThousands to tens of thousands physical First error-corrected runs on reduced finance problems. Research value only.
  3. 2031 to 2034Vendor roadmaps 1,000 to 2,000 logical qubitsOrder of magnitude Amplitude estimation at useful sizes for VaR and credit risk. Advantage still to be shown.
  4. 2035 and beyondResource estimates About 7,500 logical qubitsPhysical count depends on error rates Advantage on derivatives pricing: autocalls and TARFs in about a second.

Sources: IBM roadmap (Starling, about 200 logical qubits in 2029; Blue Jay, about 2,000 from 2033); Quantinuum (Apollo, fault-tolerant in 2029); Chakrabarti et al., Quantum 5, 463 (2021).

Spend the wait well

A quantum pricer will be judged against your best classical one. Everything that makes that benchmark strong pays off now, and it is the same work whether quantum arrives in 2032 or never.

  1. Benchmark what you run

    Independent reference values, documented errors, tests with criteria fixed in advance. The validated kits start here.

  2. Make it faster and safer

    Variance reduction, adjoint Greeks, richer models compared, findings closed. Gains you bank today.

  3. Decide with numbers

    A reduced case on a simulator, a resource estimate against your classical cost, and a dated go or no-go.
