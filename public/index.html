Where quantum stands for pricing

Quantum amplitude estimation makes the Monte Carlo error fall as 1/N instead of 1/√N. The theory is sound. The machines are not there yet: a useful advantage on derivatives pricing needs about a hundred times more error-corrected qubits than exist today.

Dozenslogical qubits demonstrated so far, on trapped ions and neutral atoms.Quantinuum, QuEra, Atom Computing, 2024 to 2026

About 200logical qubits targeted for 2029 by the leading roadmaps. Roadmaps are ambitions, not commitments.IBM Starling; Quantinuum Apollo

About 7,500logical qubits for an advantage on autocalls and TARFs, running in about a second.Chakrabarti et al., Quantum 5, 463 (2021)

Spend the wait well

A quantum pricer will be judged against your best classical one. Everything that makes that benchmark strong pays off now, and it is the same work whether quantum arrives in 2032 or never.

  1. Benchmark what you run

    Independent reference values, documented errors, tests with criteria fixed in advance. The validated kits start here.

  2. Make it faster and safer

    Variance reduction, adjoint Greeks, richer models compared, findings closed. Gains you bank today.

  3. Decide with numbers

    A reduced case on a simulator, a resource estimate against your classical cost, and a dated go or no-go.
